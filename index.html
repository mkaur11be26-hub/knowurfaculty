First-year survival tips 🧠

Go to office hoursTeachers notice who shows up. Bring one specific doubt, not “I didn’t understand anything”.
Email like a proClear subject, your name, roll number and section, then your question in two lines.
Find the cabin earlyVisit it once before you need it. Panic is not the time to search for the right block.
Ask about researchMany teachers love guiding curious juniors on projects. Ask what they work on.
Faculty details come from the official Thapar department pages and from students adding them. Always double-check cabins and timings with the department office.
